Upload tips
  • Shutterstock: upload the EPS 10 plus a JPG/PNG preview of at least 4 MP — every PNG from this tool is above 4 MP. Flat shapes only, no effects.
  • Vecteezy: upload the EPS (or SVG) plus the PNG preview, with the title and keywords from each design.
  • EPS text is live Helvetica-Bold — open in Illustrator and use Type > Create Outlines before uploading.
  • Seamless: every tape is built from one exact repeat unit. The tile file is a single unit — buyers can join copies end-to-end to print any length with no break in the stripes, chevrons or wording.
  • Originality: all designs are generated from basic shapes and generic safety words — no brand or company names.
